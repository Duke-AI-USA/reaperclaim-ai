THE PROBLEM

Where your money goes missing

Platform earnings statements are dense, inconsistent, and designed to be hard to audit. Most creators accept the number on the screen. The Reaper doesn't.

01 // DEDUCTIONS
Fees buried in fine print
Processing fees, service charges, and platform adjustments come out silently. Without a line-by-line breakdown, you're just trusting the math.
02 // ADJUSTMENTS
Retroactive clawbacks
Platforms claw back payouts for chargebacks, refunds, and policy violations — sometimes months later, often with minimal notice or explanation.
03 // COMPLEXITY
No single source of truth
Multiple revenue streams, multiple currencies, multiple pay cycles. By the time you add it up manually, something's always off — and you can't prove which line it was.
04 // DISPUTES
No evidence, no case
Even if you spot a discrepancy, platform support wants documentation. Most creators have none. The Reaper generates it for you.
